# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a welcoming and supportive environment for everyone who interacts with this project.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or hateful behavior.
- Use inclusive and positive language.
- Help create a safe environment for collaboration.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers will review and address issues in a fair and consistent manner. Violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from the project community.

## Contact
If you have any concerns or observe unacceptable behavior, please reach out to the project maintainers.
